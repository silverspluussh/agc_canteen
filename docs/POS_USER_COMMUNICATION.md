# AGCL Canteen POS — Communication to Users

> Distribute to all staff, contractors, visitors (where applicable), and canteen / POS operators ahead of go-live. This memo explains what is changing, what you must do, and what to expect at the enrollment and till points.

---

## 1. What is coming

We are preparing to implement a **one-stop solution for generating meal vouchers** across all AGCL canteen locations. This new system replaces the use of **paper vouchers** and **any other technologies that were in place before** it. Once it is live, all meal vouchers will be issued through the AGCL POS terminals using your biometric details — no paper chits, no manual tallies, no separate tracking sheets.

## 2. Biometric enrollment — required for everyone

At the appropriate time, **all staff and personnel will be required to enroll at least five (5) of their fingers**. These finger enrollments will serve as your **biometric access** for generating meal vouchers at any AGCL canteen till.

Enrolling multiple fingers is important — it gives you a reliable way to generate your voucher even if one finger has a cut, is wet, or is temporarily unreadable.

### How finger enrollment works (high level)

1. Visit your designated **enrollment point** at the canteen or with the HR/IT enrollment team.
2. Have an operator confirm your **name, staff ID, and department** before anything is captured, so the enrollment is linked to the correct profile.
3. When asked, place the required finger flat and steady on the scanner.
4. Repeat for **at least five fingers** (e.g., primary thumb + four backup fingers across both hands).
5. A short confirmation that your enrollment was received; you do not need to do anything further unless you are later notified of a problem.

If you have damaged or difficult-to-read prints on some fingers, tell the enrollment operator — they can enroll extra backup fingers.

## 3. Your meal quota

Your entitlement is set by your **type of employment** and your **shift configuration**. It determines:

- the number of meals you are entitled to **per day**, and
- a **total quota** covering your entire working period.

The POS applies your entitlement automatically:

- On any given day, you can generate vouchers up to your **daily** entitlement.
- Across your working days, you can generate vouchers up to your **total period** entitlement.

If you are unsure what your quota is, check with your department head or the canteen administrator.

## 4. Over-generated vouchers are backcharges

Any vouchers generated **beyond your entitlement** will be **recorded and reported as backcharges**. Backcharges are attributed to:

- your **department**, and
- your **name**.

Backcharges are the control mechanism that ensures the quota system is respected. They are reviewed by HR/Finance as part of reconciliation. If you believe a backcharge is an error, escalate to your department head or the canteen administrator rather than trying to offset it yourself.

## 5. What else you should know

- **Keep your details current:** your meal entitlement is tied to your staff record. Report changes in your employment status, department, or shift to HR promptly so your quota is correct.
- **Care for your fingers:** cuts, wetness, or very dirty hands can affect scanning. Clean and dry your hands before presenting your finger.
- **Lost / damaged enrollment:** contact the enrollment point to have a finger deactivated and a replacement enrolled.
- **Support:** for any POS, enrollment, or quota issue, contact the canteen administrator or IT support before assuming the system is wrong.
- **Timeline:** roll-out dates and the list of enrollment points will be communicated separately. Please attend your scheduled enrollment window.
